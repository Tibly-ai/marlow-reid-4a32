Where our work concentrates

Five areas, chosen because they overlap. A shareholder dispute is rarely only a dispute, and a lease rarely stays a lease. We handle the whole arc of the problem.

All practice areas
01

Commercial Litigation

Contract and partnership disputes, breach of fiduciary duty, and injunction practice in state and federal court.
02

Business & Transactions

Entity formation, operating agreements, buy-sell terms, and the sale or purchase of closely held companies.
