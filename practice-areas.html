Practice Areas

Five areas of counsel, handled by the partner you hired.

Marlow & Reid Law keeps a deliberately narrow practice. We advise closely held companies in Chicago and the collar counties on the work that decides how a business is owned, what it promises in writing, who it hires, where it operates, and what happens when an agreement breaks down.

Every matter is staffed by a partner from the first call through closing or judgment. If a question falls outside these five areas, we will say so and point you to someone who handles it every week.

  1. 01 Corporate Formation & Governance Entity selection, operating and shareholder agreements, founder equity, annual records.
  2. 02 Commercial Contracts Master services agreements, supply and distribution terms, licensing, vendor review.
  3. 03 Employment Matters Handbooks, classification, restrictive covenants, separations, agency responses.
  4. 04 Real Estate Transactions Commercial purchase and sale, leasing, title and survey review, loan documents.
  5. 05 Dispute Resolution Demand letters, mediation, arbitration, and trial work in state and federal court.

Scope of work

What each engagement actually covers

01 / Corporate

Corporate Formation & Governance

We form Illinois and Delaware entities and write the documents that govern them: who owns what, who decides what, and what happens when an owner leaves. Most clients come to us at formation and stay for the annual upkeep.

  • / Entity selection, filings, EIN, and registered agent setup
  • / Operating agreements, bylaws, and shareholder agreements
  • / Founder equity, vesting, and buy-sell provisions
  • / Minutes, consents, and clean-up of neglected records

02 / Contracts

Commercial Contracts

We draft the agreements a company signs every week and negotiate the ones it signs once a year. Where a client already has forms, we rewrite them into a set that is consistent, readable by a sales team, and defensible if it ever reaches a judge.

  • / Master services, statements of work, and subcontracts
  • / Supply, distribution, and reseller arrangements
  • / Software licensing, SaaS terms, and data provisions
  • / Redline review of vendor and customer paper

03 / Employment

Employment Matters

We advise employers on the decisions that carry risk: how people are classified, what they sign on day one, and how a separation is documented. Illinois wage and non-compete rules changed materially in recent years, and older handbooks usually show it.

  • / Offer letters, handbooks, and policy updates
  • / Contractor versus employee classification review
  • / Confidentiality, non-solicit, and non-compete terms
  • / Separation agreements and IDHR or EEOC responses

04 / Real Estate

Real Estate Transactions

Commercial and mixed-use work across Cook, DuPage, and Lake counties. We handle the contract, the title and survey objections, the lender's package, and the closing itself, and we tell you early when a deal has a problem worth walking away from.

  • / Purchase and sale agreements, due diligence, closings
  • / Office, retail, and industrial leases for either side
  • / Title, survey, easement, and zoning review
  • / Loan documents and landlord estoppel packages

05 / Disputes

Dispute Resolution & Litigation

Contract claims, partnership breakups, construction and mechanics lien matters, and non-compete disputes. We appear in the Circuit Court of Cook County, the Northern District of Illinois, and in AAA arbitration. Roughly two thirds of the matters we open resolve before a case is filed, which is usually the cheaper outcome for everyone.

  • / Demand letters and pre-suit negotiation
  • / Mediation and arbitration advocacy
  • / Emergency injunction and TRO practice
  • / Trial and post-judgment collection

Engagement structure

How we bill, and how long things take

Every engagement starts with a written scope and a number. If the work grows past that number, we call you before the meter runs, not after. The first thirty-minute conversation is not billed.

Practice area

Typical fee arrangement

Usual timeline

Corporate formation

Fee arrangement

Flat fee for the formation package, including filings and the governing agreement. Hourly for negotiated multi-owner terms.

Timeline

Two to four weeks from intake to signed and filed.

Commercial contracts

Fee arrangement

Flat fee per template drafted. Review and negotiation of the other side's paper is hourly with a not-to-exceed cap.

Timeline

First markup returned within five business days.

Employment matters

Fee arrangement

Monthly counsel retainer for companies that call often. Flat fee for a handbook build. Hourly for agency charges and investigations.

Timeline

Handbooks in three to four weeks. Separation papers inside 48 hours when a date is set.

Real estate

Fee arrangement

Flat transaction fee quoted against deal size and property type. Leases are quoted per document before drafting begins.

Timeline

Most closings land 30 to 60 days after contract, subject to lender pace.

Dispute resolution

Fee arrangement

Hourly against a replenishing retainer, with a monthly budget agreed in advance. Staged flat fees available through the pleading phase.

Timeline

Pre-suit resolution in 60 to 90 days. Filed cases in Cook County commonly run 9 to 18 months.

Figures above describe how we ordinarily structure work. They are not a quote, and no attorney-client relationship exists until we sign an engagement letter together.
