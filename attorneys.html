The people on your matter

Attorneys

Five lawyers, one office, and a deliberate limit on how many matters we carry at once. The attorney you meet at the first consultation is the attorney who argues the motion, negotiates the deal, and returns the call at 6pm.

Admitted in Illinois, with additional admissions in the Northern District of Illinois, the Seventh Circuit, and, for two of our attorneys, Indiana and Wisconsin.

Founding partners

Practicing together since 2004

01 / Litigation

Elena Marlow

Partner, Trial & Appellate Practice

Elena has tried thirty-one cases to verdict in state and federal court, most of them commercial disputes between closely held companies and their former owners. She spent six years at a national defense firm before opening this practice, and she still writes her own briefs. Clients hire her when a case is likely to be decided on the record rather than the handshake.

Bar admissions
Illinois; U.S. District Court, Northern District of Illinois (Trial Bar); U.S. Court of Appeals, Seventh Circuit
Education
J.D., University of Michigan Law School; B.A., Political Science, Northwestern University
Leads
Shareholder and partnership disputes, breach of contract trials, injunction practice, appeals
Ask for Elena

02 / Business Law

Daniel Reid

Partner, Corporate & Transactions

Daniel handles the work that keeps companies out of Elena's calendar: formation documents, operating agreements, purchase and sale of businesses, commercial leases, and the succession planning that founders keep postponing. He has closed more than two hundred transactions for Illinois manufacturers, medical practices, and family-owned distributors.

Bar admissions
Illinois; Indiana; U.S. District Court, Northern District of Illinois
Education
J.D., Chicago-Kent College of Law, with honors; B.S., Accountancy, University of Illinois
Leads
Entity formation, mergers and acquisitions, commercial contracts, ownership succession
